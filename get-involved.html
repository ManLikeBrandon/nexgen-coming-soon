Program Applications

Apply to NexGen

Tell us about yourself and which program area best fits your goals. We review every submission and respond by email.

If applications are closed, your submission is held for the next review round.

📝

Step 1 — Submit Your Application

Complete the form. All fields are required. Be honest — that's what we're looking for.

📞

Step 2 — Short Interview

Shortlisted applicants get a 20-minute conversation with someone from our team.

🎉

Step 3 — Welcome to NexGen

Successful applicants receive an offer, onboarding details, and a welcome pack that doesn't feel like a corporate handbook.

2026 Intake Windows

Intake 1: February to March. Intake 2: August to September. Early applications are encouraged.

Mentorship applications are reviewed manually by our core team to ensure strong student-mentor matching.
