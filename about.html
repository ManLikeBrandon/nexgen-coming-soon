Who We Are

Our Story, Mission
& Vision

The NexGen Leaders Foundation (NGL) is a non-profit company led by young professionals and emerging leaders who are committed to empowering youth in the Southern Suburbs.

How It Started

Built on a Simple Observation

NexGen Leaders Foundation began with a simple but powerful realization: there is an abundance of talent in our local schools, but a shortage of the guidance needed to unlock it.

In early 2026, the foundation was established by Amber Dreyer and Michai Mentor. They saw recurring gaps in the Southern Suburbs, where ambitious students had potential but not always a clear roadmap into higher education and professional opportunities.

What started as a shared vision for getting the underprivileged educated evolved into a structured movement that pairs mentorship, leadership development, and community support for students who need it most.

Our Focus Schools

Steenberg High School, Sibelius High School, Crestway High School, Lavender Hill High School, Heathfield High School, and Pelican Park High School.

Foundation Timeline
2019 Foundation established. First cohort of 30 young people enrolled in the Leadership Academy pilot.
2020 Mentorship Network launched. Programs moved online during the pandemic — no cohort was cancelled.
2022 Youth Impact Grants launched. Over R80 000 disbursed to 14 youth-led community projects in year one.
2024 Expanded to 15 communities. 500+ alumni. First Annual NexGen Leadership Summit draws 300+ people.
2026 Foundation established by Amber Dreyer and Michai Mentor.
2026 Mentorship and educational support launched across six core schools in the Southern Suburbs.
Now Growing a sustainable movement of youth mentorship, leadership, and community pride. Our Mission

To develop young leaders who are skilled, self-aware, and genuinely ready to build something.

Next Expanding mentorship reach and strengthening pathways from school to higher education and careers. stays active long after formal programs end.

Our Vision

Communities shaped by young leaders who know what they stand for and have the tools to act on it.

A future where every young person has access to guidance, opportunity, and a clear pathway to success, regardless of background or circumstance.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

🎯

Purpose

We ask the hard questions: what are you building, and why? Leaders without purpose make noise; we help students define their path.

🔍

Integrity

We say what we mean, do what we say, and expect the same from participants — in programs, in feedback, and in how they carry the NexGen name.

🌍

Equity

Talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not. We bridge that gap for students in the Southern Suburbs.

🤝

Community

We are more than a program; we are a network where students and young professionals keep showing up for each other.

📈

Excellence

We set high standards because we believe young people are capable of reaching them when support and accountability are in place.

🧭

Relatable Leadership

As a team of young professionals, we provide practical mentorship students can relate to, turning distant goals into real plans.
