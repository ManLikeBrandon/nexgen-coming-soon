Who We Are

Built for the People Who Come Next

We bridge the gap between potential and opportunity by providing mentorship and practical guidance that helps students excel academically and prepare for future careers.

Our targeted school impact focuses on six schools in the Southern Suburbs:

  • Steenberg High School
  • Sibelius High School
  • Crestway High School
  • Lavender Hill High School
  • Heathfield High School
  • Pelican Park High School
