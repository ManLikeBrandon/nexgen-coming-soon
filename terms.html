7. Refunds & Cancellations

Once-off donations

Because donations are voluntary gifts rather than purchases, they are generally non-refundable. We will, however, refund a donation in full where:

  • It was made in error — for example, you entered R5 000 instead of R500 — and you tell us within 30 days.
  • It was duplicated by a technical fault.
  • It was made fraudulently, without the cardholder's authority.

To request a refund, email info@nexgenleaders.org with the date, the amount, and the email address you used. We aim to review requests within 5 business days. Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method, and can take a further 5 to 10 business days to reflect, depending on your bank.

Monthly donations

You can cancel a monthly donation at any time, with no notice period and no penalty. Email info@nexgenleaders.org and we will cancel it, or cancel it yourself through PayFast. Cancelling stops all future collections. It does not automatically refund payments already collected, though the grounds above still apply to each of them.

Program fees

Where a program carries a participation fee, the refund terms for that fee will be set out in the program's own joining information before you pay.
