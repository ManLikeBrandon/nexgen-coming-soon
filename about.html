Who We Are

Our Story, Mission
& Vision

The NexGen Leaders Foundation (NGL) is a non-profit company led by young professionals and emerging leaders committed to empowering youth in the Southern Suburbs.

How It Started

Built on a Simple Observation

NexGen Leaders Foundation began with a simple but powerful realization: there is an abundance of talent in local schools, but a shortage of guidance needed to unlock it.

In early 2026, the foundation was established by Amber Dreyer and Michai Mentor. They saw recurring gaps in the Southern Suburbs, where ambitious students had potential but not always a clear roadmap into higher education and professional opportunities.

What started as a shared vision for getting the underprivileged educated evolved into a structured movement that pairs mentorship, leadership development, and community support for students who need it most.

Why We Exist

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower young people through mentorship, leadership development, and real-world exposure.

Through real programs, honest mentorship, access to funding, and a community that stays active long after formal programs end.

Our Vision

Communities shaped by young leaders who know what they stand for and have the tools to act on it.

A future where every young person has access to guidance, opportunity, and a clear pathway to success, regardless of background or circumstance.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

🎯

Purpose

We ask the hard questions: what are you building, and why? Leaders without purpose make noise; we help students define their path.

🔍

Integrity

We say what we mean, do what we say, and expect the same from participants — in programs, in feedback, and in how they carry the NexGen name.

🌍

Equity

Talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not. We bridge that gap for students in the Southern Suburbs.

🤝

Community

We are more than a program; we are a network where students and young professionals keep showing up for each other.

📈

Excellence

We set high standards because we believe young people are capable of reaching them when support and accountability are in place.

🧭

Relatable Leadership

As a team of young professionals, we provide practical mentorship students can relate to, turning distant goals into real plans.
